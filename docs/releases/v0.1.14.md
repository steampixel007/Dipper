## Changelog

- Refresh Dipper's visual design with a calmer minimalist layout, cleaner surfaces, and unified typography across light and dark themes.
- Enrich the browse anime detail sidebar with broadcast start dates, available subtitle groups, Bangumi and official site links, direct access to the episode library for followed anime, and cached detail reuse.
- Redesign watch-version setup into a focused single-column flow: pick a recommended version, choose whether to download aired episodes or follow future updates only, and expand per-episode details on demand.
- Speed up entering watch-version setup by reusing cached detail previews to choose a Chinese-subtitled default group immediately, displaying version summaries in the group picker, and loading full history only for truncated groups.
- Improve version recommendations to prefer mainstream episode coverage over one-off specials, grade compatibility without rejecting untagged MP4 or HEVC/MKV-only groups, warn about non-revision same-episode release collisions, and remember your subtitle language and preference choices.
- Upgrade original-title setup into a two-step pick-by-example flow that extracts distinguishing keywords automatically from a sample release and lets you download matched existing episodes alongside following future updates.

## 更新日志

- 焕新界面视觉风格，在浅色与夜间模式下提供更克制的极简排版、层级与控件体验。
- 丰富浏览页的番剧详情侧栏：展示开播日期、可用字幕组列表、Bangumi 与官网链接，已追番支持直达剧集库，并复用已缓存的详情数据。
- 将「选择观看版本」重构为聚焦单列流程：先确认推荐版本，再决定是否立即下载已播出集数，逐集明细与原始文件名改为按需展开。
- 加快进入观看版本的首屏加载：复用详情页预览数据秒选更合适的中文字幕组、在更换字幕组列表展示版本速览，仅在需要时拉取完整历史。
- 优化版本推荐算法：主力连载集数优先于偶发单集版本，兼容性改为综合打分（不再因未标编码或仅提供 HEVC/MKV 而无推荐），同集出现不同发布时主动提醒，并记住上次选择的字幕语言与偏好。
- 将「按原标题自选」升级为两步「照着一条选」流程：点选一条代表样本即可自动提取区分关键词，并支持在追番同时直接下载已匹配的已有集数。
