## Changelog

- Support custom upload limits, seeding time, and share ratios with an explicit unlimited option.
- Add a dedicated Tracker manager for searching, adding, and deleting trackers, importing online tracker lists, restoring the built-in XIU2 list, and deduplicating addresses across sources.
- Add a download concurrency limit, with excess tasks queued until a download slot opens.
- Show the default source as line one and let custom proxies include an address, username, and password.
- Keep anime browsing focused on discovery by removing download counts from posters, and prevent season navigation past the current year.

## 更新日志

- 自定义上传上限、分享时长和分享率，并支持明确选择“不限”。
- 新增独立的 Tracker 管理页，可搜索、添加和删除 Tracker，导入在线清单，恢复内置 XIU2 清单，并自动去除不同清单中的重复地址。
- 新增下载并发上限，超出并发数的任务会自动排队，有空位时继续下载。
- 默认线路显示为线路一；自定义代理支持填写地址、用户名和密码。
- 番剧浏览更专注于发现内容：海报卡片不再显示下载完成数，季节切换不会越过当前年份。
